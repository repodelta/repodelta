You receive one JSON input bundle. Treat that bundle as the complete evidence
universe. Do not use prior knowledge of RepoDelta, a generated report,

or any unstated implementation claim.

For every candidate membership, decide its semantic relation to its authored
Requirement or Guardrail from the authored contract and exact reviewed diff or
source evidence. The labels mean:

- `implements`: directly realizes a Requirement.
- `constrains`: directly enforces or preserves a Guardrail.

- `contextual_support`: related support, not a direct semantic realization.
- `unrelated`: no supported semantic relation.
- `insufficient`: the bounded evidence cannot responsibly decide.

- `direct_capable` with `explicit_authoring`, `typed_predicate`,

  relevance is plausible but lacks an authoritative deterministic basis.

`insufficient`, use `insufficient` and `none`. A semantic-direct label must
cite at least one stable source URL from its candidate's `sources` list. Do not

## What this proves

- The candidate wording makes provenance, authority scope/owner, authorized
  dependencies, conditional obligations, and material re-planning observable in
  the same case matrix.
- The candidate distinguishes domain, evaluation-local, and
  presentation-local outputs without promoting local projections to shared
  authority.
- The candidate's explicit helper/layout exemption reduces noise on a held-out
  near-miss.
- The candidate catches all three declared material re-plan cases in this
  replay without false-positive re-plans.

## What this does not prove

- No live coding agent was run under both documents.
- Recognition and re-plan metrics are rubric replay metrics, not behavioral
  telemetry.
- The synthetic execution-time injection is declared evidence, not a historical
  runtime trace.
- Seven cases cannot establish statistical generalization or cost impact.

The next independent study should run real agent sessions against held-out
repositories and score the same dimensions without changing the frozen corpus
labels.
